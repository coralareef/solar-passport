HOW TO READ SOLAR PASSPORT

Plain-English terms

Self-use

Solar electricity your building uses immediately.

Export

Extra solar electricity sent to the grid.

Payback

Approximate years for project cash savings to recover the investment.

IRR

An annualised investment-return measure. Useful for comparing investments; it is not a guarantee.

NPV

The present value of future project cash flows after applying a discount rate.

kWp

The rated DC size of the solar panel system.

Building Passport is preliminary decision support. Structural certification, electrical design, utility approval, legal advice and financing approval remain with the relevant professionals and authorities.
